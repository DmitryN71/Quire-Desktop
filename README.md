Quire Desktop brings native notifications and enhanced productivity to your Quire.io workflow. Stay on top of your tasks with real-time alerts, due date reminders, and a smart morning digest—all delivered directly to your desktop.

Getting Started:

First, download and install the desktop application from here (note for Edge/Windows users: You may see a security warning. This is normal for new apps. Click "See more" → "Keep anyway" to proceed).
Then click “Install” here to authorize the app with your Quire account
Key Features:

Native Notifications – Get instant alerts for task assignments, comments, due dates, and status changes
Morning Digest – Start your day with a summary of tasks due today
Seamless Integration – One-click navigation from notifications directly to tasks in Quire
Offline-Ready – Syncs automatically when you're back online
Privacy-Focused – Secure OAuth authentication with no data collection
Perfect for teams and individuals who want desktop-native task management without keeping browser tabs open. Quire Desktop keeps you focused and informed throughout your workday.
